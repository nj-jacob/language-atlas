## 2026-11-07
- Tool box, the last five tools, all built to the Ontario curriculum, the Science of Reading and Orton-Gillingham. The tool box now has 9 tools on 5 shelves. **Word shades** (Words and meaning): put word cards on a paint strip from weaker to stronger (tiny, small, big, huge), and try each in a sentence; Grades 7 and 8 add the same meaning with a different feeling (stubborn, firm, determined). **Word web**: one word with what it means, words like it, opposites, its word family and an example, and from Grade 7 its Greek or Latin roots (trans + port); Cover hides each part so a grown-up can ask first. **Text maps** (Reading): read a short text, then sort its parts onto a map (Beginning, middle, end; Sequence; Story map; Main idea; Story mountain; Cause and effect; Compare; Fact or opinion; Argument), or type on a blank map for any book. **Paragraph builder** (Writing): build a paragraph from a topic sentence, three details and a closing sentence, add joining words, spot the sentence that does not belong; stretch a sentence with because, but and so; write your own with starters. **Letter pad**: printing (abc, ABC, 123) and cursive from Grade 3 on sky, plane, grass and ground lines, with numbered start dots, Trace, Copy, Remember and Show me (draws each stroke in order); write with a finger or a pencil. New checks for all five, including every letter measured on its lines.
- Tool box: **Reading window** (Tools, then Reading), for fluency (B2.8: accuracy, pace and expression), built to the Science of Reading and Orton-Gillingham. The child reads each grade's own passages aloud, shortest first. Line shows one sentence at a time with the rest faded, like a reading ruler; Phrase puts a scoop under each phrase (phrase-cued text); Page shows the whole passage. Music marks how to say it (pause at a comma, voice down at a full stop, up at a question, strong at "!", talking tinted). Tap a word to make it a tricky word for later practice; Read again counts rereads; bigger text and extra spacing. Starts by grade: Line with big text in Grades 1 to 3, Phrase in 4 to 6, Page in 7 and 8. In grown-up view, a one-minute read gives words correct per minute and accuracy, saved to My progress (grown-up only). Nothing is read aloud to the child and nothing is scored for the child. New check: every passage is cut into phrases without losing a word, and the windows and one-minute read work on a phone, iPad and laptop.
- Word builder, Beats: **syllable types**, built to the Science of Reading and Orton-Gillingham. Each beat shows its type (closed, open, silent e, vowel team, with r, consonant-le; endings such as -tion from Grade 4) and plays the vowel sound that type gives, so a cut in the wrong place shows straight away (tig-er or ti-ger). Mark vowels puts v over the vowels and c over the consonants between them. From Grade 2 each cut shows its pattern (VC/CV rabbit, V/CV tiger, VC/V camel, V/V lion, ostrich, consonant-le, two words) and the strong beat, with a grown-up tip on flexing and the quiet /uh/ beat. Find a type lists the words with that type. The Beats word list grows from 67 to 286 words (102 in Grade 1), with every type and pattern in every grade, Canadian words (maple, harbour, Saskatchewan, Manitoba) and longer words by grade. New check: every syllable's type is worked out a second way and must agree.
- Tool box: **Sound board** (Tools, then Sounds and letters; now the first tool). Built to the Science of Reading and Orton-Gillingham: sound first, then its spellings, and see it, hear it, say it, move it. Wall: the consonants by where the mouth makes them (lips to throat) and how the air comes out, with quiet and voiced partners side by side, and the vowels in a vowel valley (smile, open, round) with the sliding vowels and vowels with r. Tap a sound to hear Noel's recording and see a side-view mouth picture, every way to spell it up to the grade, a key word and, in grown-up view, a tip. Move it: push one chip for each sound in a picture word, turn on letters, and change one sound. Cards: the sound drill in teaching order, with flip, Hear it first, Shuffle and My deck. 30 new two-tone key-word pictures (bat, goat, igloo, octopus, whale and more). New check: every sound is recorded, pictured and spelled, and the board is used like a child would on a phone, iPad and laptop.

## 2026-11-06
- Tool box, second tool: **Sentence builder** (Tools, then Sentences). Word cards coloured by their job (who or what, does, where or when, how, joins, marks) for free play, alone or with a grown-up. Build a sentence, pick a card to move, swap or take it out, and see ticks for who or what, does and a mark at the end (from Grade 3, whether it stands alone, and a note when two sentences are crammed together). Moves: Join two and Split (and, but, because, when ...; a semicolon from Grade 7), To the front (a where-or-when part or a because part, with its comma, from Grade 3), Make it passive or active (Grade 6+), Clauses (marks each part that can or cannot stand alone), Mix it up, New sentence, and Show jobs off for a grown-up to ask what each card does. New check: every example sentence is complete, and every move gives the sentence back when it is undone the other way, on a phone, iPad and laptop.
- Word builder: silent-e words. New vowel tiles a-e, i-e, o-e, u-e (and e-e from Grade 2) put their e after the next letter, so c, a-e, k spells cake; the word shows the vowel and its e joined, and Blend plays the long vowel. 23 new words (cake, bike, home, cube, flute, theme and more).
- Tool box, first tool: **Word builder** (Tools in the header, `#tools`). One word in three linked views for free play, alone or with a grown-up: Sounds (tap letter tiles into sound boxes, swap one sound, Blend plays Noel's recorded sounds in order), Beats (tap between letters to cut a word into syllables, then show where the beats go) and Parts (prefixes, bases and suffixes with what each part means, the spelling rules and the word family; Greek and Latin roots from Grade 7). Each grade gets the tiles and words for its grade and the ones before. New check: every word in the word bank can be built and is spelled right by the rules, and Word builder is used like a child would on a phone, iPad and laptop.
