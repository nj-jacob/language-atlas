# Changelog

## 2026-11-01
- Deeper Grade 5: all 14 chapters now meet the full-chapter standard. Every idea has two worked examples, 3 "Together" and 5 "On your own" questions, and its own practice skill asked in 4 or more ways (74 skills where there were 29), with bigger banks so practice does not repeat. Reading for Meaning gains 8 new original texts at Grade 5 length (17 in all, each with pictures) and 13 "Read and answer" sets; Thinking Critically gains short opinion and inclusion texts. Every new question shape has its own answer check.
- Every Grade 3 Language chapter is now a full chapter, like the Reading for Meaning pilot: 73 ideas, each with two worked examples, 3 "Together" and 5 "On your own" questions, and its own practice skill asked in 4 or more ways (73 skills, up from 34). Thinking About Texts adds a "Think about a text" set; 15 passages gain clues to work out, causes and effects, and the letter writers' opinions. New ideas: words for other subjects (B2.2) and quoting from a text (B3.3).
- Deeper Grade 2: all 14 chapters now meet the full-chapter standard. 80 practice skills instead of 30 (one per idea, each asked in 4 or more ways, each with 30 or more different questions), two worked examples in every "Watch me", 3 "Together" and 5 "On your own" questions per idea, 19 original reading passages instead of 7 (11 at Grade 2 length, each with a picture), and "Read and answer" sets in Reading. Old practice progress for the replaced Grade 2 skills starts fresh.
- Deeper Grade 3 "Reading for Meaning", the pilot for making every chapter a real study tool: 16 new original passages (23 in all, 9 of them at Grade 3 length, each with a picture), 8 practice skills instead of 3 (one per idea, each asked in 4 ways), two worked examples in every "Watch me", 3 "Together" and 5 "On your own" questions per idea, and new "Read and answer" sets (read a whole text, then answer 5 questions about it).
- Deeper Grade 7: all 14 chapters now meet the full-chapter standard (`full: true`). 85 ideas, each with two worked examples, 3 "Together" and 5 "On your own" questions, and its own practice skill (85 skills, each asked in 4 or more ways, with 30 or more different questions). 16 original Grade 7 passages with pictures (`grade7/texts.js`, now using `Atlas.passages`), "Read and answer" sets in Reading for Meaning, and much larger banks everywhere: 19 Latin and Greek roots, 29 context clues, 16 phrased sentences, 20 joined ideas, 12 fragments and 10 run-ons, 8 model paragraphs, 12 media techniques and more. Activities with a grown-up are unchanged.
- Engine: tap the sentence in the text, main idea and story map pictures (`Atlas.W.web`, `Atlas.W.chain`), passages in paragraphs with a picture band, 15 new line-art pictures, practice never repeats a question the child has just seen, and "Got it" needs 4 right first time when an idea has 5 "On your own" questions. A new check holds chapters marked `full: true` to the full standard. Passage helpers are shared in the engine (`Atlas.passages`), so every grade's `texts.js` uses one copy.

## 2026-10-31
- Letter sounds: Noel's 44 recorded sounds (`assets/sounds/`) now play from sound buttons in the Grade 1 and Grade 2 phonics lessons and questions (`Atlas.W.sound`, `Atlas.W.soundRow`). The checks make sure every button has its file and none sits inside an answer choice.
- Full Grade 2: 14 chapters, 80 ideas, 30 practice skills and 34 activities with a grown-up, covering all 61 Grade 2 expectations in strands A, B, C and D (checked against the 2023 curriculum and the Language Foundations Continuum: soft c and g, uni-/bi-/tri-, mis-/pre-, -er/-or/-ness/-y, group nouns, -er/-est, similes and consonance, contents pages and charts). Seven original reading passages (`grade2/texts.js`). Linked from the home page.
- One app: Language is now part of Atlas, at ontario-math-atlas.web.app/language/, with the same sign-in as Math. Inside Atlas, "All grades" opens the Atlas Curriculum page, the Math link stays on the same site, and a signed-in student's progress saves to their family account so it shows on the parent page. The separate Language site, its Firebase settings and the preview links are gone. Publishing now asks math-atlas to publish the whole site (one-time setup in SETUP.md).
- Foundation: shared look matching Math Atlas, shared header (All grades, Math Atlas link, text size, grown-up view), one shared engine (lessons in three steps, adaptive practice, chapter quiz, check-up, progress page, six answer types), automatic checks, GitHub workflows (checks, previews, publish), Firebase hosting as a second site in the Math Atlas project, and docs.
- Grade 1 placeholder with one demo chapter: capital letters and end marks (B3.3).
- Lessons show more and say less: "Watch me" shows one step at a time (a picture and one short line), new word pictures (`words.js`: word-part tiles, picture cues, sentence strips), and a new "build a word" answer type. Grade 1 demo text shortened.
- Grade 3 demo chapter: the prefixes un- and re- (B2.3, B2.4), with 3 ideas and 2 practice skills that each ask in 4 ways.
- Full Grade 3: 13 chapters, 71 ideas, 29 practice skills and 31 activities with a grown-up, covering all 61 Grade 3 expectations in strands A, B, C and D. Seven original reading passages (`grade3/texts.js`).
- Engine: activities with a grown-up (`tasks.js`): a picture and one line for the child, an optional box to write in, and a four-level rubric the grown-up marks in grown-up view. The marks fill in the check-up map. Also new word pictures (syllables, blanks, passages with headings, more picture cues) and quick question makers (`Atlas.Q.one`, `Atlas.Q.tiles`, `Atlas.Q.all`).
- Calmer screens: on phones and tablets the header is one slim row with a Menu button (grades, chapters, Math Atlas, text size, grown-up view). An idea or a practice skill gets the whole screen: a back link, progress dots, the question, its picture and its answers. Expectation codes show only in grown-up view. The step buttons and labels became one small line, such as "Together · 1 of 2".
- Say it once: an idea's title is its rule, and nothing on the screen repeats it. "Watch me" lines add something new or are left out (for example, a second example instead of restating the rule), the Key idea box moved to a short recap on the end-of-lesson page, the step buttons sit on one row, and lesson and practice buttons below the card show only where needed. On a computer the header is one row, with text size and grown-up view under "Settings". A new check fails if a line or recap repeats the title. Grade 1 and Grade 3 lessons tightened to match.
- Engine: 23 line-art picture cues for words children name by sound (`Atlas.W.pic('sun')`), for Grades 1 and 2 phonics.
- Engine: long answer cards wrap instead of running off a phone screen; new picture cues `before` and `wrong` (for pre- and mis-).
- Full Grade 1: 15 chapters, 86 ideas, 33 practice skills and 35 activities with a grown-up, covering all 63 Grade 1 expectations in strands A, B, C and D (replaces the Grade 1 demo). Phonics follows the Language Foundations Continuum (sounds in words, letters, short and long vowels, sh ch th wh ck, spelling patterns). Six original reading passages (`grade1/texts.js`). Picture cues in the sounds, phonics and pictures lessons. The grown-up says the sounds until recorded clips are added.
