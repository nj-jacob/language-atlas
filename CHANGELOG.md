# Changelog

## 2026-10-31
- One app: Language is now part of Atlas, at ontario-math-atlas.web.app/language/, with the same sign-in as Math. Inside Atlas, "All grades" opens the Atlas Curriculum page, the Math link stays on the same site, and a signed-in student's progress saves to their family account so it shows on the parent page. The separate Language site, its Firebase settings and the preview links are gone. Publishing now asks math-atlas to publish the whole site (one-time setup in SETUP.md).
- Foundation: shared look matching Math Atlas, shared header (All grades, Math Atlas link, text size, grown-up view), one shared engine (lessons in three steps, adaptive practice, chapter quiz, check-up, progress page, six answer types), automatic checks, GitHub workflows (checks, previews, publish), Firebase hosting as a second site in the Math Atlas project, and docs.
- Grade 1 placeholder with one demo chapter: capital letters and end marks (B3.3).
- Lessons show more and say less: "Watch me" shows one step at a time (a picture and one short line), new word pictures (`words.js`: word-part tiles, picture cues, sentence strips), and a new "build a word" answer type. Grade 1 demo text shortened.
- Grade 3 demo chapter: the prefixes un- and re- (B2.3, B2.4), with 3 ideas and 2 practice skills that each ask in 4 ways.
- Full Grade 3: 13 chapters, 71 ideas, 29 practice skills and 31 activities with a grown-up, covering all 61 Grade 3 expectations in strands A, B, C and D. Seven original reading passages (`grade3/texts.js`).
- Engine: activities with a grown-up (`tasks.js`): a picture and one line for the child, an optional box to write in, and a four-level rubric the grown-up marks in grown-up view. The marks fill in the check-up map. Also new word pictures (syllables, blanks, passages with headings, more picture cues) and quick question makers (`Atlas.Q.one`, `Atlas.Q.tiles`, `Atlas.Q.all`).
- Calmer screens: on phones and tablets the header is one slim row with a Menu button (grades, chapters, Math Atlas, text size, grown-up view). An idea or a practice skill gets the whole screen: a back link, progress dots, the question, its picture and its answers. Expectation codes show only in grown-up view. The step buttons and labels became one small line, such as "Together · 1 of 2".
- Say it once: an idea's title is its rule, and nothing on the screen repeats it. "Watch me" lines add something new or are left out (for example, a second example instead of restating the rule), the Key idea box moved to a short recap on the end-of-lesson page, the step buttons sit on one row, and lesson and practice buttons below the card show only where needed. On a computer the header is one row, with text size and grown-up view under "Settings". A new check fails if a line or recap repeats the title. Grade 1 and Grade 3 lessons tightened to match.
- Engine: 23 line-art picture cues for words children name by sound (`Atlas.W.pic('sun')`), for Grades 1 and 2 phonics.
- Full Grade 1: 15 chapters, 86 ideas, 33 practice skills and 35 activities with a grown-up, covering all 63 Grade 1 expectations in strands A, B, C and D (replaces the Grade 1 demo). Phonics follows the Language Foundations Continuum (sounds in words, letters, short and long vowels, sh ch th wh ck, spelling patterns). Six original reading passages (`grade1/texts.js`). Picture cues in the sounds, phonics and pictures lessons. The grown-up says the sounds until recorded clips are added.
- Full Grade 5: 14 chapters, 74 ideas, 29 practice skills and 26 activities with a grown-up, covering all 59 Grade 5 expectations in strands A, B, C and D. Nine original reading passages (`grade5/texts.js`), answer checks in `tests/verify/grade5-*.js`, and a Grade 5 card on the home page.
